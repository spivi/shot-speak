# Shot options for: "the bullet time thingy where the camera swishes around them mid-air"

**What your words mean in film language:**

- 'Bullet time' is a frozen-moment arc: time almost stops while the camera moves.
- 'Swishy' is a fast move with motion blur: a whip pan, a fast arc or a crash zoom.
- 'Around them' is an arc shot. A partial arc is safer than a full circle.
- Air moments read best from a low camera, with a real height and real gravity.

Subject: two masked ninjas at the top of a jump off a low wall. Place: a walled courtyard at sunset. Frame: 16:9.

Note: Options A, D slow time down. In a finished piece, use slow motion for 1 beat only.

## Option A: Frozen-moment arc (bullet time)  (recommended)

**Why recommended:** It is the closest match to your words. Best for: one peak moment in the whole piece: the top of a jump, the instant before a landing or a hit.

**In plain words:** Time almost stops and the camera sweeps part of the way around the subject.
**What the viewer feels:** The viewer gets a held breath: one instant, seen from more than one side.
**Pro term to learn:** Frozen-moment arc (bullet time)
**Choose it when:** One peak moment in the whole piece: the top of a jump, the instant before a landing or a hit.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a fast, smooth arc of about 130 degrees around the subject: it starts behind the shoulder, passes the profile and stops at a front three-quarter view; it never reaches a view straight in front |
| Camera placement | at the subject's own height, the lens level, 2 to 3 m away, on one side of the action for the whole move |
| Lens | 35 mm. Wide enough to feel the camera travel past the background, not so wide that bodies bend at the frame edge. |
| Light | One hard low sun, camera facing into the light: A single low, hard sun behind the subject. The camera looks toward it. |
| Colour grade | Film print, deep blacks: Looks like real film: fine grain, true black shadows, natural colour. |
| Speed | Frozen moment: Time almost stops while the camera keeps moving. |
| Length | 2 to 3 s |

**AI look it avoids:** 180 or 360 degree orbit; Floating, weightless bodies.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- A full 180 or 360 degree orbit turns faces into the lens and flips left and right on screen. Ask for about 130 degrees.
- Generators let frozen bodies float upward. Write 'real gravity, drifting only a hand's width'.
- The background behind the subject is invented during the move. Keep the place simple.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text

Avoid: the camera never completes a half circle and never reaches a view straight in front of the face; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays.
```

## Option B: Arc shot (partial orbit, real time)

**In plain words:** The camera swings around the subject at normal speed, fast, and stops.
**What the viewer feels:** Energy and swagger. The camera has an attitude of its own.
**Pro term to learn:** Arc shot (partial orbit, real time)
**Choose it when:** An entrance, a trick, a turn: any moment that needs speed more than awe.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | one fast arc of about 90 degrees around the subject in real time, starting on a whip of motion blur and stopping dead on a side-on frame |
| Camera placement | at waist height, 2 m away, the lens level, on one side of the action |
| Lens | 24 mm. A wide lens close to the subject makes the swing feel fast and keeps the place in the frame. |
| Light | Low hard side sun: Late sun from one side. One half of everything is lit, one half is dark. |
| Colour grade | Warm natural: Warm sunlight colour, but real: no orange wash over everything. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 1.5 to 3 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Aquarium speed: slow floating camera and slow bodies; 180 or 360 degree orbit.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators slow an arc to a gentle drift. Write 'fast', 'real time' and where it stops.
- A wide lens stretches faces near the frame edge. Keep faces near the middle third.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
Two masked ninjas at the top of a jump off a low wall; in real time the camera whips one fast arc of about 90 degrees around them at waist height, close on a wide lens, and stops dead side-on; 24 mm lens; low, hard late-afternoon sun from one side only; one side of the subject lit, the other side in real shadow; long hard-edged shadows across the ground; warm natural grade: warm highlights where the sun lands, neutral shadows, natural colours, true blacks; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. ## Option C: Speed ramp

**In plain words:** Normal speed, a short slow-down at the best instant, then normal speed again.
**What the viewer feels:** The viewer feels the speed and then gets a clear look at the best instant.
**Pro term to learn:** Speed ramp
**Choose it when:** Sport, tricks, a jump, a throw: fast action with one peak.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a side-on tracking move that travels with the subject at their speed |
| Camera placement | side-on at hip height, 4 m away, travelling parallel to the action |
| Lens | 50 mm. A normal lens side-on keeps body shapes true while the speed change does the work. |
| Light | Low hard side sun: Late sun from one side. One half of everything is lit, one half is dark. |
| Colour grade | Cool and desaturated: Cold, quiet, a little grey-blue. Serious. |
| Speed | Speed ramp: Normal speed, a short slow-down at the peak, then back to normal speed. |
| Length | 3 to 5 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Slow motion everywhere; Centred, front-facing subject.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators stay slow after the ramp. Write 'back to real time before the landing'.
- Say exactly which instant is slow.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
Two masked ninjas at the top of a jump off a low wall, tracked side-on at hip height; real time into the move, a short ramp to slow motion at the peak, then back to real time; 50 mm lens; low, hard late-afternoon sun from one side only; one side of the subject lit, the other side in real shadow; long hard-edged shadows across the ground; cool desaturated grade: grey-blue shadows, neutral skin, low saturation, soft contrast with true blacks; real time into the move, a short ramp to slow motion at the peak of the action, then back to real time before the landing or the contact. Avoid: real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it; not a centred front-facing subject; the subject is off-centre, seen in profile, three-quarter or from behind; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays.

## Option D: Low-angle wide shot

**In plain words:** The camera sits on the floor with a wide lens and looks along the ground. The subject towers.
**What the viewer feels:** Scale and power. The subject is bigger than the viewer.
**Pro term to learn:** Low-angle wide shot
**Choose it when:** A landing, an entrance, the top of a jump. It makes a modest real jump look heroic.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | locked: the camera does not move, the action comes to it |
| Camera placement | on the floor, 3 m away, the lens level and looking along the ground into the light |
| Lens | 18 mm. An ultra-wide lens on the floor makes the ground rush to the lens and gives a small jump real scale. |
| Light | Silhouette with a rim: The subject is a dark shape against a bright opening, with a thin edge of light. |
| Colour grade | Bleach bypass: Harsh and silvery: strong contrast, washed-out colour. |
| Speed | Slow motion (one beat): The moment is stretched. Use it for one moment in the whole piece. |
| Length | 2 to 4 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Eye-level medium shot as the default; The 35 to 50 mm default.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Do not tilt the lens up at the face: it bends bodies and generators stretch them. Keep the lens level.
- Slow motion plus a jump invites floating. Write a real height and real gravity.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
Two masked ninjas at the top of a jump off a low wall, seen from a locked camera on the floor with an ultra-wide lens, the lens level, looking along the ground into the light; 18 mm lens; the subject as a near-black silhouette against one bright opening, a thin hard rim of light on the edge of the body, the shape readable, more than a third of the frame in deep shadow; bleach-bypass look: high contrast, low saturation, silvery highlights, dense blacks, visible grain; slow motion for this one beat only; real gravity, weight and cloth move truthfully, nothing floats. Avoid: not a default eye-level medium shot; no soft even glow; one motivated light source with a real dark side; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays.
