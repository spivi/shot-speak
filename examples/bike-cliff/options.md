# Shot options for: "10 seconds of a cool action scene: a guy rides a bike over a cliff, ditches the bike mid-air and does a superhero landing"

**What your words mean in film language:**

- The look you named is, in film language: comic-book hero moment. The camera is on the ground. The person is large against the sky. The sun is behind them. Their edge is bright and their front is dark. Time slows for 1 beat at the peak. Then the speed is real again.
- Air moments read best from a low camera, with a real height and real gravity.
- Power comes from a low camera and a wide lens, not from a centred pose.

Subject: a young man in a grey hoodie and jeans rides a mountain bike off a cliff edge, lets go of the bike in mid-air and lands on the ground below in a superhero landing. Place: a rocky desert canyon at late afternoon. Frame: 16:9.

Note: Options A, D slow time down. In a finished piece, use slow motion for 1 beat only.

## Option A: Low-angle wide shot  (recommended)

**Why recommended:** It is the look you named, as decisions: comic-book hero moment. The camera is on the ground. The person is large against the sky.

**In plain words:** The camera sits on the floor with a wide lens and looks along the ground. The subject towers.
**What the viewer feels:** Scale and power. The subject is bigger than the viewer.
**Pro term to learn:** Low-angle wide shot
**Choose it when:** A landing, an entrance, the top of a jump. It makes a modest real jump look heroic.

**What makes that look (comic-book hero moment):**
- The camera is on the ground. The person is large against the sky.
- The sun is behind them. Their edge is bright and their front is dark.
- Time slows for 1 beat at the peak. Then the speed is real again.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | locked: the camera does not move, the action comes to it |
| Camera placement | on the floor, 3 m away, the lens level and looking along the ground into the light |
| Lens | 18 mm. An ultra-wide lens on the floor makes the ground rush to the lens and gives a small jump real scale. |
| Light | One hard low sun, camera facing into the light: A single low, hard sun behind the subject. The camera looks toward it. |
| Colour grade | Bleach bypass: Harsh and silvery: strong contrast, washed-out colour. |
| Speed | Slow motion (one beat): The moment is stretched. Use it for one moment in the whole piece. |
| Length | 2 to 4 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Eye-level medium shot as the default; The 35 to 50 mm default.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Do not tilt the lens up at the face: it bends bodies and generators stretch them. Keep the lens level.
- Slow motion plus a jump invites floating. Write a real height and real gravity.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text

Avoid: not a default eye-level medium shot; no soft even glow; one motivated light source with a real dark side; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays.
```

## Option B: Speed ramp

**In plain words:** Normal speed, a short slow-down at the best instant, then normal speed again.
**What the viewer feels:** The viewer feels the speed and then gets a clear look at the best instant.
**Pro term to learn:** Speed ramp
**Choose it when:** Sport, tricks, a jump, a throw: fast action with one peak.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a side-on tracking move that travels with the subject at their speed |
| Camera placement | side-on at hip height, 4 m away, travelling parallel to the action |
| Lens | 50 mm. A normal lens side-on keeps body shapes true while the speed change does the work. |
| Light | Flat overcast daylight: A grey sky. Even, honest light with soft shadows under things. |
| Colour grade | Cool and desaturated: Cold, quiet, a little grey-blue. Serious. |
| Speed | Speed ramp: Normal speed, a short slow-down at the peak, then back to normal speed. |
| Length | 3 to 5 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Slow motion everywhere; Centred, front-facing subject.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators stay slow after the ramp. Write 'back to real time before the landing'.
- Say exactly which instant is slow.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
A young man in a grey hoodie and jeans rides a mountain bike off a cliff edge, lets go of the bike in mid-air and lands on the ground below in a superhero landing, tracked side-on at hip height; real time into the move, a short ramp to slow motion at the peak, then back to real time; 50 mm lens; flat overcast daylight from a grey sky, soft contact shadows under bodies and objects, muted natural contrast, no sun; cool desaturated grade: grey-blue shadows, neutral skin, low saturation, soft contrast with true blacks; real time into the move, a short ramp to slow motion at the peak of the action, then back to real time before the landing or the contact. Avoid: real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it; not a centred front-facing subject; the subject is off-centre, seen in profile, three-quarter or from behind; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays.

## Option C: Crane up (rising reveal)

**In plain words:** The camera starts low behind something and rises to show the whole place.
**What the viewer feels:** Arrival. The world opens.
**Pro term to learn:** Crane up (rising reveal)
**Choose it when:** The first view of a place, or the last shot of a story.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | one rise: the camera starts low behind a foreground object and lifts straight up to reveal the place beyond the subject |
| Camera placement | behind the subject, starting at knee height and ending 4 m up |
| Lens | 24 mm. A wide lens makes the rise feel large and shows the place it reveals. |
| Light | Low hard side sun: Late sun from one side. One half of everything is lit, one half is dark. |
| Colour grade | Warm natural: Warm sunlight colour, but real: no orange wash over everything. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 4 to 6 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Centred, front-facing subject; Aquarium speed: slow floating camera and slow bodies.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators invent the revealed place. Describe what is beyond in one clear sentence.
- It becomes a slow drift. Give it a start, an end and a time.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
The camera rises from knee height behind a young man in a grey hoodie and jeans rides a mountain bike off a cliff edge, lets go of the bike in mid-air and lands on the ground below in a superhero landing, straight up to 4 m, and reveals the place beyond them; the subject keeps their back to the camera; 24 mm lens; low, hard late-afternoon sun from one side only; one side of the subject lit, the other side in real shadow; long hard-edged shadows across the ground; warm natural grade: warm highlights where the sun lands, neutral shadows, natural colours, true blacks; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: not a centred front-facing subject; the subject is off-centre, seen in profile, three-quarter or from behind; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.

## Option D: Frozen-moment arc (bullet time)

**In plain words:** Time almost stops and the camera sweeps part of the way around the subject.
**What the viewer feels:** The viewer gets a held breath: one instant, seen from more than one side.
**Pro term to learn:** Frozen-moment arc (bullet time)
**Choose it when:** One peak moment in the whole piece: the top of a jump, the instant before a landing or a hit.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a fast, smooth arc of about 130 degrees around the subject: it starts behind the shoulder, passes the profile and stops at a front three-quarter view; it never reaches a view straight in front |
| Camera placement | at the subject's own height, the lens level, 2 to 3 m away, on one side of the action for the whole move |
| Lens | 35 mm. Wide enough to feel the camera travel past the background, not so wide that bodies bend at the frame edge. |
| Light | One hard low sun, camera facing into the light: A single low, hard sun behind the subject. The camera looks toward it. |
| Colour grade | Film print, deep blacks: Looks like real film: fine grain, true black shadows, natural colour. |
| Speed | Frozen moment: Time almost stops while the camera keeps moving. |
| Length | 2 to 3 s |

**AI look it avoids:** 180 or 360 degree orbit; Floating, weightless bodies.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- A full 180 or 360 degree orbit turns faces into the lens and flips left and right on screen. Ask for about 130 degrees.
- Generators let frozen bodies float upward. Write 'real gravity, drifting only a hand's width'.
- The background behind the subject is invented during the move. Keep the place simple.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
A young man in a grey hoodie and jeans rides a mountain bike off a cliff edge, lets go of the bike in mid-air and lands on the ground below in a superhero landing; time almost stops while the camera sweeps a fast, smooth arc of about 130 degrees around them at their own height, lens level, from behind the shoulder past the profile to a front three-quarter view; 35 mm lens; one hard low sun as the only strong light, behind the subject; the camera faces into the light; bright rims on hair and shoulders, long shadows that fall toward the lens, about a third of the frame in deep true shadow; photochemical film look: fine grain, deep true blacks with detail, natural skin and material colours, restrained saturation; time almost stops: the subject drifts only a hand's width while the camera moves fast; real gravity returns at the end of the move. Avoid: the camera never completes a half circle and never reaches a view straight in front of the face; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays.
```
