# Shot options for: "dramatic like the dark family crime drama look dinner scene"

**What your words mean in film language:**

- The look you named is, in film language: dark family crime drama. The light comes from straight above. The eyes sink into shadow, so the viewer cannot read the person. The colour is warm amber and the picture is dark. Most of the room is black. The camera is still, or it moves forward very slowly. Power sits and waits.
- 'Moody' is mostly light: one hard source and real shadow.
- 'Dramatic' is not one technique. It is a choice of camera position, lens, light and speed.

## Option A: Push-in (dolly in)  (recommended)

**Why recommended:** It is the look you named, as decisions: dark family crime drama. The light comes from straight above. The eyes sink into shadow, so the viewer cannot read the person.

**In plain words:** The whole camera moves toward the subject. The background slides and grows too.
**What the viewer feels:** The viewer leans in. A decision or a realisation is happening.
**Pro term to learn:** Push-in (dolly in)
**Choose it when:** A character decides, understands or hides something. The most useful 'dramatic zoom'.

**What makes that look (dark family crime drama):**
- The light comes from straight above. The eyes sink into shadow, so the viewer cannot read the person.
- The colour is warm amber and the picture is dark. Most of the room is black.
- The camera is still, or it moves forward very slowly. Power sits and waits.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a steady push-in: the camera itself travels toward the subject for the whole shot, from a medium view to a chest-up view |
| Camera placement | at the subject's eye height, from the side at a three-quarter angle, starting 3 m away |
| Lens | 50 mm. A normal lens keeps the face natural while the camera does the work. This is a decision here, not a default: the move carries the feeling. |
| Light | Pool of top light: One lamp above. A circle of light on the floor, darkness around it. |
| Colour grade | Film print, deep blacks: Looks like real film: fine grain, true black shadows, natural colour. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 3 to 5 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Centred, front-facing subject; Soft, even golden glow.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators mix up a push-in and a zoom. Write 'the camera travels toward them, the background slides past'.
- From the front it becomes the standard AI frame. Push in from the side.

## Option B: Locked-off wide (tableau)

**In plain words:** The camera does not move at all. The frame is composed like a painting and the action happens inside it.
**What the viewer feels:** Calm and control. The viewer has time to look.
**Pro term to learn:** Locked-off wide (tableau)
**Choose it when:** Establishing a place, quiet moments, dry comedy, a pause after action.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | locked: no movement at all for the whole shot |
| Camera placement | far back at waist height, the lens level, with a dark foreground shape on one side |
| Lens | 35 mm. Moderately wide and level keeps lines straight, so the composition holds like a painting. |
| Light | Soft window light from one side: A large window on one side. Gentle, but still with a dark side. |
| Colour grade | Muted earth tones: Browns, greys and dull greens. Worn and real. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 4 to 8 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Aquarium speed: slow floating camera and slow bodies; Symmetrical hero framing.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators add a slow drift to every shot. Write 'locked camera, no movement at all'.
- Without a foreground shape and a dark area it becomes a flat postcard.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
A locked camera, no movement at all: a composed wide frame from waist height with a dark foreground shape on one side, an old man at the head of a long table listening without a word small and off-centre; 35 mm lens; soft daylight from one large window on one side; the far side of the face falls into natural shadow; no fill light, the room behind stays darker than the subject; muted earth-tone grade: browns, greys and dull greens, low saturation, soft highlights, worn surfaces; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. ## Option C: Long-lens profile close-up
```

**In plain words:** A tight shot from far away, from the side. The face fills the frame and the background melts.
**What the viewer feels:** Tension and focus. The viewer reads the thought on the face.
**Pro term to learn:** Long-lens profile close-up
**Choose it when:** The beat before the action, a reaction, a stare-down. For two people, use 2 matched profile shots and cut between them.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | locked, or a very slow push-in of a few centimetres |
| Camera placement | exactly side-on, at the subject's chest height, 6 m away, never in front of the face |
| Lens | 85 mm. A long lens from a distance compresses the space and isolates the face without the wide-angle bend. |
| Light | Blade of light: A narrow strip of hard light crosses the eyes. The rest falls into shadow. |
| Colour grade | Black and white: No colour. Shape, light and texture do the work. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 1 to 3 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Centred, front-facing subject; Eyes into the lens.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators turn the face toward the camera. Write 'in profile, the camera never in front'.
- Ask for the eyes to move, not the head, or the profile is lost.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
A chest-up shot of an old man at the head of a long table in clean profile, listening without a word, from a long lens far away at chest height, the camera never in front of the face; only the eyes move; 85 mm lens; a dark space with one narrow hard-edged blade of light that crosses the eyes; the rest of the face and body falls into deep shadow; the light comes from one visible gap or window; black and white with a full tonal range: true black, clean white, fine grain, strong shapes; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. ## Option D: Rack focus (focus pull)
```

**In plain words:** The sharp part of the picture moves from one thing to another.
**What the viewer feels:** The viewer's attention is moved by hand: 'first this, now that'.
**Pro term to learn:** Rack focus (focus pull)
**Choose it when:** To connect an object and a person, or a person and what they see.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | locked camera; the focus moves once, from a near object to the subject behind it |
| Camera placement | close behind a foreground object at its own height, the subject 4 m beyond it, seen three-quarter |
| Lens | 85 mm. A long lens has a thin sharp zone, so the change of focus is clear. |
| Light | Night, practical lights only: Only the lamps that exist in the place: a street light, a sign, a screen. |
| Colour grade | Neon noir: Night colours from signs and lamps against black. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 2 to 4 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Fake creamy bokeh, or everything in focus; Aquarium speed: slow floating camera and slow bodies.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Many generators cannot time a focus change. Ask for it once, with a clear order: 'first the object sharp, then the person sharp'.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
Locked camera on a long lens: first a near object is sharp and an old man at the head of a long table is soft behind it, listening without a word; then the focus moves once to the subject; 85 mm lens; night; the only light comes from sources visible in the place, each with its own colour and a short reach; large areas of true darkness between them; night grade with two saturated practical colours against true black, colour only where a light source reaches; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: no cut-out subject on a smeared background; focus falls off naturally with distance; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.
```
