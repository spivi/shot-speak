# Shot options for: "make it feel epic but sad"

**What your words mean in film language:**

- The look is not clear yet. The words name a mood (epic, sad), not a picture. A mood has many possible shots. 2 or more moods pull in different directions. The look must be found with the user. Find it with the user in rounds of 2 to 4 mock stills that differ in 1 decision, then lock it.
- 'Dramatic' is not one technique. It is a choice of camera position, lens, light and speed.
- Loneliness is a small subject in a large frame.

Subject: a lone hiker reaching the top of a ridge. Place: a mountain ridge in cloud. Frame: 16:9.

## Option A: Pull-back (dolly out)  (recommended)

**Why recommended:** It is the closest match to your words. Best for: an ending, a loss, a reveal of where the subject really is.

**In plain words:** The camera travels away from the subject and shows how much is around them.
**What the viewer feels:** The subject gets smaller. Loneliness, or the size of the task.
**Pro term to learn:** Pull-back (dolly out)
**Choose it when:** An ending, a loss, a reveal of where the subject really is.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a steady pull-back: the camera travels away from the subject in a straight line, from a tight view to a wide view |
| Camera placement | at the subject's eye height, three-quarter from behind, ending 12 m away |
| Lens | 35 mm. Moderately wide, so the growing space is felt without bending the subject at the start. |
| Light | Flat overcast daylight: A grey sky. Even, honest light with soft shadows under things. |
| Colour grade | Cool and desaturated: Cold, quiet, a little grey-blue. Serious. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 4 to 6 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Centred, front-facing subject; Symmetrical hero framing.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators change the subject during a long pull-back. Keep them still.
- Describe what enters the frame, or it fills with random objects.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
A lone hiker reaching the top of a ridge; the camera travels steadily away from them in a straight line, from a tight view to a wide view, until they are small in a large empty place; 35 mm lens; flat overcast daylight from a grey sky, soft contact shadows under bodies and objects, muted natural contrast, no sun; cool desaturated grade: grey-blue shadows, neutral skin, low saturation, soft contrast with true blacks; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: not a centred front-facing subject; the subject is off-centre, seen in profile, three-quarter or from behind; no posed symmetrical hero framing; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.
```

## Option B: Long-lens profile close-up

**In plain words:** A tight shot from far away, from the side. The face fills the frame and the background melts.
**What the viewer feels:** Tension and focus. The viewer reads the thought on the face.
**Pro term to learn:** Long-lens profile close-up
**Choose it when:** The beat before the action, a reaction, a stare-down. For two people, use 2 matched profile shots and cut between them.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | locked, or a very slow push-in of a few centimetres |
| Camera placement | exactly side-on, at the subject's chest height, 6 m away, never in front of the face |
| Lens | 85 mm. A long lens from a distance compresses the space and isolates the face without the wide-angle bend. |
| Light | Blade of light: A narrow strip of hard light crosses the eyes. The rest falls into shadow. |
| Colour grade | Film print, deep blacks: Looks like real film: fine grain, true black shadows, natural colour. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 1 to 3 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Centred, front-facing subject; Eyes into the lens.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators turn the face toward the camera. Write 'in profile, the camera never in front'.
- Ask for the eyes to move, not the head, or the profile is lost.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
A chest-up shot of a lone hiker in clean profile, reaching the top of a ridge, from a long lens far away at chest height, the camera never in front of the face; only the eyes move; 85 mm lens; a dark space with one narrow hard-edged blade of light that crosses the eyes; the rest of the face and body falls into deep shadow; the light comes from one visible gap or window; photochemical film look: fine grain, deep true blacks with detail, natural skin and material colours, restrained saturation; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: not a centred front-facing subject; the subject is off-centre, seen in profile, three-quarter or from behind; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.
```

## Option C: Push-in (dolly in)

**In plain words:** The whole camera moves toward the subject. The background slides and grows too.
**What the viewer feels:** The viewer leans in. A decision or a realisation is happening.
**Pro term to learn:** Push-in (dolly in)
**Choose it when:** A character decides, understands or hides something. The most useful 'dramatic zoom'.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a steady push-in: the camera itself travels toward the subject for the whole shot, from a medium view to a chest-up view |
| Camera placement | at the subject's eye height, from the side at a three-quarter angle, starting 3 m away |
| Lens | 50 mm. A normal lens keeps the face natural while the camera does the work. This is a decision here, not a default: the move carries the feeling. |
| Light | Soft window light from one side: A large window on one side. Gentle, but still with a dark side. |
| Colour grade | Film print, deep blacks: Looks like real film: fine grain, true black shadows, natural colour. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 3 to 5 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Centred, front-facing subject; Soft, even golden glow.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators mix up a push-in and a zoom. Write 'the camera travels toward them, the background slides past'.
- From the front it becomes the standard AI frame. Push in from the side.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
A lone hiker reaching the top of a ridge; the camera itself travels steadily toward them from a three-quarter side position at eye height, from a medium view to chest-up, the background sliding past; 50 mm lens; soft daylight from one large window on one side; the far side of the face falls into natural shadow; no fill light, the room behind stays darker than the subject; photochemical film look: fine grain, deep true blacks with detail, natural skin and material colours, restrained saturation; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: not a centred front-facing subject; the subject is off-centre, seen in profile, three-quarter or from behind; no soft even glow; one motivated light source with a real dark side; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.
```

## Option D: Locked-off wide (tableau)

**In plain words:** The camera does not move at all. The frame is composed like a painting and the action happens inside it.
**What the viewer feels:** Calm and control. The viewer has time to look.
**Pro term to learn:** Locked-off wide (tableau)
**Choose it when:** Establishing a place, quiet moments, dry comedy, a pause after action.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | locked: no movement at all for the whole shot |
| Camera placement | far back at waist height, the lens level, with a dark foreground shape on one side |
| Lens | 35 mm. Moderately wide and level keeps lines straight, so the composition holds like a painting. |
| Light | Soft window light from one side: A large window on one side. Gentle, but still with a dark side. |
| Colour grade | Muted earth tones: Browns, greys and dull greens. Worn and real. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 4 to 8 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Aquarium speed: slow floating camera and slow bodies; Symmetrical hero framing.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators add a slow drift to every shot. Write 'locked camera, no movement at all'.
- Without a foreground shape and a dark area it becomes a flat postcard.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
A locked camera, no movement at all: a composed wide frame from waist height with a dark foreground shape on one side, a lone hiker reaching the top of a ridge small and off-centre; 35 mm lens; soft daylight from one large window on one side; the far side of the face falls into natural shadow; no fill light, the room behind stays darker than the subject; muted earth-tone grade: browns, greys and dull greens, low saturation, soft highlights, worn surfaces; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; no posed symmetrical hero framing; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.
```
