# Shot options for: "my kid lands a skateboard trick and I want it to look like a skate video"

**What your words mean in film language:**

- The look you named is, in film language: fisheye skate video. An extremely wide lens is very low and very close. The ground rushes past. Straight lines bend at the edges of the frame. The light is plain daylight. The camera rides along with the person.
- A hit, a catch or a landing is a contact. Show the move wide, then the contact tight, at full speed from the contact.
- Air moments read best from a low camera, with a real height and real gravity.

Subject: a kid on a skateboard landing a kickflip off a low kerb. Place: an empty car park on a grey day. Frame: 16:9.

## Option A: Low-angle wide shot  (recommended)

**Why recommended:** It is the look you named, as decisions: fisheye skate video. An extremely wide lens is very low and very close. The ground rushes past.

**In plain words:** The camera sits on the floor with a wide lens and looks along the ground. The subject towers.
**What the viewer feels:** Scale and power. The subject is bigger than the viewer.
**Pro term to learn:** Low-angle wide shot
**Choose it when:** A landing, an entrance, the top of a jump. It makes a modest real jump look heroic.

**What makes that look (fisheye skate video):**
- An extremely wide lens is very low and very close. The ground rushes past.
- Straight lines bend at the edges of the frame.
- The light is plain daylight. The camera rides along with the person.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | locked: the camera does not move, the action comes to it |
| Camera placement | on the floor, 3 m away, the lens level and looking along the ground into the light |
| Lens | 18 mm. An ultra-wide lens on the floor makes the ground rush to the lens and gives a small jump real scale. |
| Light | Flat overcast daylight: A grey sky. Even, honest light with soft shadows under things. |
| Colour grade | Muted earth tones: Browns, greys and dull greens. Worn and real. |
| Speed | Slow motion (one beat): The moment is stretched. Use it for one moment in the whole piece. |
| Length | 2 to 4 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Eye-level medium shot as the default; The 35 to 50 mm default.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Do not tilt the lens up at the face: it bends bodies and generators stretch them. Keep the lens level.
- Slow motion plus a jump invites floating. Write a real height and real gravity.

## Option B: Follow shot (from behind)

**In plain words:** The camera walks or runs behind the subject, close to their shoulder.
**What the viewer feels:** The viewer is with the subject and discovers the place at the same time.
**Pro term to learn:** Follow shot (from behind)
**Choose it when:** Entering a place, a chase, a walk into trouble.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a follow move close behind the subject at their speed, with the natural small bounce of a carried camera |
| Camera placement | behind one shoulder at shoulder height, 1 m back |
| Lens | 24 mm. A wide lens close behind the shoulder shows where the subject is going. |
| Light | Night, practical lights only: Only the lamps that exist in the place: a street light, a sign, a screen. |
| Colour grade | Neon noir: Night colours from signs and lamps against black. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 4 to 8 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Centred, front-facing subject; Eyes into the lens.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators swing round to show the face. Write 'the camera stays behind the shoulder for the whole shot'.
- Long corridors change shape over time. Keep it under 8 s.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
The camera follows close behind one shoulder of a kid on a skateboard landing a kickflip off a low kerb, at their speed, with a small natural bounce, and stays behind the shoulder for the whole shot; 24 mm lens; night; the only light comes from sources visible in the place, each with its own colour and a short reach; large areas of true darkness between them; night grade with two saturated practical colours against true black, colour only where a light source reaches; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: not a centred front-facing subject; the subject is off-centre, seen in profile, three-quarter or from behind; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.
```

## Option C: Speed ramp

**In plain words:** Normal speed, a short slow-down at the best instant, then normal speed again.
**What the viewer feels:** The viewer feels the speed and then gets a clear look at the best instant.
**Pro term to learn:** Speed ramp
**Choose it when:** Sport, tricks, a jump, a throw: fast action with one peak.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a side-on tracking move that travels with the subject at their speed |
| Camera placement | side-on at hip height, 4 m away, travelling parallel to the action |
| Lens | 50 mm. A normal lens side-on keeps body shapes true while the speed change does the work. |
| Light | Flat overcast daylight: A grey sky. Even, honest light with soft shadows under things. |
| Colour grade | Cool and desaturated: Cold, quiet, a little grey-blue. Serious. |
| Speed | Speed ramp: Normal speed, a short slow-down at the peak, then back to normal speed. |
| Length | 3 to 5 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Slow motion everywhere; Centred, front-facing subject.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators stay slow after the ramp. Write 'back to real time before the landing'.
- Say exactly which instant is slow.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
A kid on a skateboard landing a kickflip off a low kerb, tracked side-on at hip height; real time into the move, a short ramp to slow motion at the peak, then back to real time; 50 mm lens; flat overcast daylight from a grey sky, soft contact shadows under bodies and objects, muted natural contrast, no sun; cool desaturated grade: grey-blue shadows, neutral skin, low saturation, soft contrast with true blacks; real time into the move, a short ramp to slow motion at the peak of the action, then back to real time before the landing or the contact. Avoid: real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it; not a centred front-facing subject; the subject is off-centre, seen in profile, three-quarter or from behind; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays.
```

## Option D: Tracking shot (lateral dolly)

**In plain words:** The camera travels sideways with the subject, like a car driving beside them.
**What the viewer feels:** Momentum. The viewer travels with the subject.
**Pro term to learn:** Tracking shot (lateral dolly)
**Choose it when:** Running, riding, walking with purpose.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a lateral tracking move at the subject's speed, things in the foreground wiping past the lens |
| Camera placement | side-on at waist height, 5 m away, with posts, people or plants passing between camera and subject |
| Lens | 85 mm. A long lens makes the foreground and background rush past, so the move feels fast. |
| Light | Flat overcast daylight: A grey sky. Even, honest light with soft shadows under things. |
| Colour grade | Warm natural: Warm sunlight colour, but real: no orange wash over everything. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 3 to 6 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Aquarium speed: slow floating camera and slow bodies; Centred, front-facing subject.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators drift ahead of or behind the subject. Write 'the subject stays on the same third of the frame'.
- Keep one screen direction: left to right or right to left, for the whole sequence.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
A kid on a skateboard landing a kickflip off a low kerb, tracked side-on from a long lens at their speed, the subject held on the same third of the frame while foreground shapes wipe past; 85 mm lens; flat overcast daylight from a grey sky, soft contact shadows under bodies and objects, muted natural contrast, no sun; warm natural grade: warm highlights where the sun lands, neutral shadows, natural colours, true blacks; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; not a centred front-facing subject; the subject is off-centre, seen in profile, three-quarter or from behind; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.
```
