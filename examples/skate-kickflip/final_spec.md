Picked: option A. Mixed in: none.

## Watch for

- Do not tilt the lens up at the face: it bends bodies and generators stretch them. Keep the lens level.
- Slow motion plus a jump invites floating. Write a real height and real gravity.

## Prompt block

```text
SHOT: Low-angle wide shot. 16:9 frame, 2 to 4 s.
SUBJECT AND ACTION: a kid on a skateboard landing a kickflip off a low kerb, in an empty car park on a grey day.
CAMERA MOVE: locked: the camera does not move, the action comes to it.
CAMERA PLACEMENT: on the floor, 3 m away, the lens level and looking along the ground into the light.
LENS: 18 mm.
LIGHT: flat overcast daylight from a grey sky, soft contact shadows under bodies and objects, muted natural contrast, no sun.
COLOUR: muted earth-tone grade: browns, greys and dull greens, low saturation, soft highlights, worn surfaces.
SPEED: slow motion for this one beat only; real gravity, weight and cloth move truthfully, nothing floats.
LOOK: an extremely wide lens very low and very close to the wheels, the ground rushing past the bottom of the frame, lines bending at the edges, plain daylight.
RULES: State the exact number of people. Screen direction stays the same for the whole shot.
AVOID: not a default eye-level medium shot; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; worn real textures with dust, creases and small defects, nothing plastic or perfectly clean.
```
