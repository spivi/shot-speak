# Shot options for: "grandma blows out the candles, zoom in slowly, make it warm and emotional"

**What your words mean in film language:**

- 'Zoom in' can mean 4 different things: a push-in, a crash zoom, a dolly zoom or a true slow zoom.
- Loneliness is a small subject in a large frame.

Subject: an old woman blowing out the candles on a birthday cake. Place: a small dark kitchen. Frame: 16:9.

## Option A: Push-in (dolly in)  (recommended)

**Why recommended:** It is the closest match to your words. Best for: a character decides, understands or hides something. The most useful 'dramatic zoom'.

**In plain words:** The whole camera moves toward the subject. The background slides and grows too.
**What the viewer feels:** The viewer leans in. A decision or a realisation is happening.
**Pro term to learn:** Push-in (dolly in)
**Choose it when:** A character decides, understands or hides something. The most useful 'dramatic zoom'.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a steady push-in: the camera itself travels toward the subject for the whole shot, from a medium view to a chest-up view |
| Camera placement | at the subject's eye height, from the side at a three-quarter angle, starting 3 m away |
| Lens | 50 mm. A normal lens keeps the face natural while the camera does the work. This is a decision here, not a default: the move carries the feeling. |
| Light | Firelight: A low warm source that flickers. Faces lit from below or the side. |
| Colour grade | Film print, deep blacks: Looks like real film: fine grain, true black shadows, natural colour. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 3 to 5 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Centred, front-facing subject; Soft, even golden glow.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators mix up a push-in and a zoom. Write 'the camera travels toward them, the background slides past'.
- From the front it becomes the standard AI frame. Push in from the side.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
An old woman blowing out the candles on a birthday cake; the camera itself travels steadily toward them from a three-quarter side position at eye height, from a medium view to chest-up, the background sliding past; 50 mm lens; one low warm source, a fire or a single lamp, lighting faces from the side; the light falls off fast into darkness; photochemical film look: fine grain, deep true blacks with detail, natural skin and material colours, restrained saturation; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: not a centred front-facing subject; the subject is off-centre, seen in profile, three-quarter or from behind; no soft even glow; one motivated light source with a real dark side; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.
```

## Option B: Crash zoom (snap zoom)

**In plain words:** The lens punches in on the subject in a fraction of a second.
**What the viewer feels:** A punch, a shock or a joke. It feels like a news camera caught it.
**Pro term to learn:** Crash zoom (snap zoom)
**Choose it when:** A sudden discovery, a comic beat, a hit.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a crash zoom: the camera does not travel, the lens snaps from a wide view to a tight view in about a third of a second, with a small handheld shake at the end |
| Camera placement | far away, 8 m or more, at shoulder height, three-quarter to the subject |
| Lens | 24 mm zooming to 85 mm. The zoom itself is the effect: the background does not slide, the frame simply slams tighter. |
| Light | Flat overcast daylight: A grey sky. Even, honest light with soft shadows under things. |
| Colour grade | Bleach bypass: Harsh and silvery: strong contrast, washed-out colour. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 1 to 2 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Aquarium speed: slow floating camera and slow bodies; Eye-level medium shot as the default.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators turn a zoom into a slow push-in. Write 'the camera does not travel' and the time: a third of a second.
- Used twice, it becomes a tic.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
An old woman blowing out the candles on a birthday cake; from far away the lens crash-zooms from a wide view to a tight three-quarter view in a third of a second, the camera itself does not travel, a small shake at the end; 24 mm zooming to 85 mm lens; flat overcast daylight from a grey sky, soft contact shadows under bodies and objects, muted natural contrast, no sun; bleach-bypass look: high contrast, low saturation, silvery highlights, dense blacks, visible grain; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; not a default eye-level medium shot; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.
```

## Option C: Pull-back (dolly out)

**In plain words:** The camera travels away from the subject and shows how much is around them.
**What the viewer feels:** The subject gets smaller. Loneliness, or the size of the task.
**Pro term to learn:** Pull-back (dolly out)
**Choose it when:** An ending, a loss, a reveal of where the subject really is.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a steady pull-back: the camera travels away from the subject in a straight line, from a tight view to a wide view |
| Camera placement | at the subject's eye height, three-quarter from behind, ending 12 m away |
| Lens | 35 mm. Moderately wide, so the growing space is felt without bending the subject at the start. |
| Light | Cold moonlight: One cold, hard source from high up. Most of the frame is dark. |
| Colour grade | Cool and desaturated: Cold, quiet, a little grey-blue. Serious. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 4 to 6 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Centred, front-facing subject; Symmetrical hero framing.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators change the subject during a long pull-back. Keep them still.
- Describe what enters the frame, or it fills with random objects.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
An old woman blowing out the candles on a birthday cake; the camera travels steadily away from them in a straight line, from a tight view to a wide view, until they are small in a large empty place; 35 mm lens; one cold hard light from high on one side, like a full moon; hard-edged shadows, most of the frame dark, details held in the blacks; cool desaturated grade: grey-blue shadows, neutral skin, low saturation, soft contrast with true blacks; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: not a centred front-facing subject; the subject is off-centre, seen in profile, three-quarter or from behind; no posed symmetrical hero framing; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.
```

## Option D: Locked-off wide (tableau)

**In plain words:** The camera does not move at all. The frame is composed like a painting and the action happens inside it.
**What the viewer feels:** Calm and control. The viewer has time to look.
**Pro term to learn:** Locked-off wide (tableau)
**Choose it when:** Establishing a place, quiet moments, dry comedy, a pause after action.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | locked: no movement at all for the whole shot |
| Camera placement | far back at waist height, the lens level, with a dark foreground shape on one side |
| Lens | 35 mm. Moderately wide and level keeps lines straight, so the composition holds like a painting. |
| Light | Soft window light from one side: A large window on one side. Gentle, but still with a dark side. |
| Colour grade | Muted earth tones: Browns, greys and dull greens. Worn and real. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 4 to 8 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Aquarium speed: slow floating camera and slow bodies; Symmetrical hero framing.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators add a slow drift to every shot. Write 'locked camera, no movement at all'.
- Without a foreground shape and a dark area it becomes a flat postcard.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
A locked camera, no movement at all: a composed wide frame from waist height with a dark foreground shape on one side, an old woman blowing out the candles on a birthday cake small and off-centre; 35 mm lens; soft daylight from one large window on one side; the far side of the face falls into natural shadow; no fill light, the room behind stays darker than the subject; muted earth-tone grade: browns, greys and dull greens, low saturation, soft highlights, worn surfaces; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; no posed symmetrical hero framing; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.
```
