# Final shot spec: Contact insert with a ramp on contact

Picked: option B. Mixed in: none.

**UNLOCKED: decisions may still change.**

| Decision | Final |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | locked and tight on the point of contact; at the frame of contact the frame kicks a hand's width in the direction of the force, then settles |
| Camera placement | exactly side-on to the line of the force, at chest height, 2 m away, never in front of a face |
| Lens | 85 mm. A long lens from the side isolates the contact and keeps the camera out of the action. |
| Light | Night, practical lights only |
| Colour grade | Bleach bypass |
| Speed | Ramp on contact |
| Length | 1.5 to 2.5 s |

## Watch for

- Generators add a slow wind-up. Write 'the first visible motion is the action itself'.
- The contact goes soft or misses. Name the part that strikes, the exact target and the reaction along the line of the force.
- Generators stretch the whole shot into slow motion. State the frame where real speed returns.

## Prompt block

```text
SHOT: Contact insert with a ramp on contact. 16:9 frame, 1.5 to 2.5 s.
SUBJECT AND ACTION: two stunt fighters in plain dark clothes trading blows, one hard right hook lands on the jaw, in a narrow brick alley at night.
CAMERA MOVE: locked and tight on the point of contact; at the frame of contact the frame kicks a hand's width in the direction of the force, then settles.
CAMERA PLACEMENT: exactly side-on to the line of the force, at chest height, 2 m away, never in front of a face.
LENS: 85 mm.
LIGHT: night; the only light comes from sources visible in the place, each with its own colour and a short reach; large areas of true darkness between them.
COLOUR: bleach-bypass look: high contrast, low saturation, silvery highlights, dense blacks, visible grain.
SPEED: slow motion on the approach, then real speed from the exact frame of contact: the reaction happens at full speed and the shot ends when the reaction ends.
RULES: State the exact number of people. Screen direction stays the same for the whole shot.
AVOID: real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; no soft even glow; one motivated light source with a real dark side; worn real textures with dust, creases and small defects, nothing plastic or perfectly clean.
```
