# Shot options for: "two guys fight in an alley and I want to feel every punch, raw and shaky"

**What your words mean in film language:**

- A hit, a catch or a landing is a contact. Show the move wide, then the contact tight, at full speed from the contact.
- 'Shaky' or 'raw' is handheld coverage, close to the action.

Subject: two stunt fighters in plain dark clothes trading blows, one hard right hook lands on the jaw. Place: a narrow brick alley at night. Frame: 16:9.

## Option A: Handheld close coverage  (recommended)

**Why recommended:** It is the closest match to your words. Best for: arguments, fights, panic, documentary truth.

**In plain words:** A camera on the shoulder, close to the action, a little rough.
**What the viewer feels:** It is happening now and nobody planned it.
**Pro term to learn:** Handheld close coverage
**Choose it when:** Arguments, fights, panic, documentary truth.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | handheld: small, nervous reframing that reacts a moment late to the action |
| Camera placement | close, 1.5 m away, at chest height, three-quarter to the subject, with a shoulder or an object soft in the foreground |
| Lens | 35 mm. Close and moderately wide puts the viewer inside the event without bending faces. |
| Light | Night, practical lights only: Only the lamps that exist in the place: a street light, a sign, a screen. |
| Colour grade | Muted earth tones: Browns, greys and dull greens. Worn and real. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 2 to 5 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Aquarium speed: slow floating camera and slow bodies; Perfect, clean textures.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators produce either no shake or an earthquake. Write 'small, late reframing'.
- Chest height from the front is the AI default. Keep the three-quarter angle and a foreground shape.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
Handheld and close: two stunt fighters in plain dark clothes trading blows, one hard right hook lands on the jaw, seen from a three-quarter position past a soft foreground shoulder, with small, late reframing; 35 mm lens; night; the only light comes from sources visible in the place, each with its own colour and a short reach; large areas of true darkness between them; muted earth-tone grade: browns, greys and dull greens, low saturation, soft highlights, worn surfaces; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; worn real textures with dust, creases and small defects, nothing plastic or perfectly clean; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.
```

## Option B: Contact insert with a ramp on contact

**In plain words:** A tight shot of the instant of contact. Slow on the way in, full speed from the contact.
**What the viewer feels:** The viewer feels the contact, not only sees it.
**Pro term to learn:** Contact insert with a ramp on contact
**Choose it when:** One decisive contact: a hit, a catch, a landing, a ball on a bat. Show the whole move in a wider shot first.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | locked and tight on the point of contact; at the frame of contact the frame kicks a hand's width in the direction of the force, then settles |
| Camera placement | exactly side-on to the line of the force, at chest height, 2 m away, never in front of a face |
| Lens | 85 mm. A long lens from the side isolates the contact and keeps the camera out of the action. |
| Light | Night, practical lights only: Only the lamps that exist in the place: a street light, a sign, a screen. |
| Colour grade | Bleach bypass: Harsh and silvery: strong contrast, washed-out colour. |
| Speed | Ramp on contact: Slow on the way in. Full speed from the instant of contact. |
| Length | 1.5 to 2.5 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Slow motion everywhere; Aquarium speed: slow floating camera and slow bodies.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Generators add a slow wind-up. Write 'the first visible motion is the action itself'.
- The contact goes soft or misses. Name the part that strikes, the exact target and the reaction along the line of the force.
- Generators stretch the whole shot into slow motion. State the frame where real speed returns.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
A tight contact insert: two stunt fighters in plain dark clothes trading blows, one hard right hook lands on the jaw, exactly side-on to the line of the force, the frame kicking a hand's width with the force at the contact; 85 mm lens; night; the only light comes from sources visible in the place, each with its own colour and a short reach; large areas of true darkness between them; bleach-bypass look: high contrast, low saturation, silvery highlights, dense blacks, visible grain; slow motion on the approach, then real speed from the exact frame of contact: the reaction happens at full speed and the shot ends when the reaction ends. Avoid: real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays.
```

## Option C: Low-angle wide shot

**In plain words:** The camera sits on the floor with a wide lens and looks along the ground. The subject towers.
**What the viewer feels:** Scale and power. The subject is bigger than the viewer.
**Pro term to learn:** Low-angle wide shot
**Choose it when:** A landing, an entrance, the top of a jump. It makes a modest real jump look heroic.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | locked: the camera does not move, the action comes to it |
| Camera placement | on the floor, 3 m away, the lens level and looking along the ground into the light |
| Lens | 18 mm. An ultra-wide lens on the floor makes the ground rush to the lens and gives a small jump real scale. |
| Light | Silhouette with a rim: The subject is a dark shape against a bright opening, with a thin edge of light. |
| Colour grade | Film print, deep blacks: Looks like real film: fine grain, true black shadows, natural colour. |
| Speed | Slow motion (one beat): The moment is stretched. Use it for one moment in the whole piece. |
| Length | 2 to 4 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Eye-level medium shot as the default; The 35 to 50 mm default.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Do not tilt the lens up at the face: it bends bodies and generators stretch them. Keep the lens level.
- Slow motion plus a jump invites floating. Write a real height and real gravity.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
Two stunt fighters in plain dark clothes trading blows, one hard right hook lands on the jaw, seen from a locked camera on the floor with an ultra-wide lens, the lens level, looking along the ground into the light; 18 mm lens; the subject as a near-black silhouette against one bright opening, a thin hard rim of light on the edge of the body, the shape readable, more than a third of the frame in deep shadow; photochemical film look: fine grain, deep true blacks with detail, natural skin and material colours, restrained saturation; slow motion for this one beat only; real gravity, weight and cloth move truthfully, nothing floats. Avoid: not a default eye-level medium shot; no soft even glow; one motivated light source with a real dark side; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays.
```

## Option D: Whip pan (swish pan)

**In plain words:** The camera snaps sideways so fast that the picture streaks, then lands on the subject.
**What the viewer feels:** A jolt. It says 'look here, now'.
**Pro term to learn:** Whip pan (swish pan)
**Choose it when:** To join two moments or two places with energy. The blur also hides a cut.

| Decision | This option |
|---|---|
| Camera movement | a whip pan: the camera snaps sideways in a streak of motion blur and lands locked on the subject |
| Camera placement | on a fixed spot at shoulder height, side-on to the action, 4 m away |
| Lens | 35 mm. Wide enough for a clear streak, tight enough that the landing frame has a subject. |
| Light | Low hard side sun: Late sun from one side. One half of everything is lit, one half is dark. |
| Colour grade | Warm natural: Warm sunlight colour, but real: no orange wash over everything. |
| Speed | Real time: Normal speed. Fast things are fast. |
| Length | 1 to 2 s |

**AI look it avoids:** Aquarium speed: slow floating camera and slow bodies; Centred, front-facing subject.

**Risks with AI video generators:**
- Some generators cannot hold a subject through the blur. Use the whip as the cut point between two clips.
- State where the pan lands, or it wanders.

**Say this to a video model:**

```text
The camera whip-pans in a streak of motion blur and lands locked on two stunt fighters in plain dark clothes trading blows, one hard right hook lands on the jaw, seen side-on; 35 mm lens; low, hard late-afternoon sun from one side only; one side of the subject lit, the other side in real shadow; long hard-edged shadows across the ground; warm natural grade: warm highlights where the sun lands, neutral shadows, natural colours, true blacks; real time for the whole shot, fast and crisp, no slow motion. Avoid: no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; not a centred front-facing subject; the subject is off-centre, seen in profile, three-quarter or from behind; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; real time, with slow motion only where the shot states it.
```
