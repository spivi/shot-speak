# Sequence: Contact beat

1 contact shown 2 times: the whole move in a medium shot, then the contact in a tight shot. The cut comes at the end of the reaction.

Hero shot: option B. Length: 10 s. Cuts at: 1.5, 4, 6, 8 s.

| # | Time | Shot | Camera | What happens | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 to 1.5 s | full shot, 35 mm | locked, side-on at waist height, both sides of the action in the frame | both men still, one on each side of the frame, eyes on each other | real time |
| 2 | 1.5 to 4 s | medium shot, 50 mm | locked, side-on at chest height, both people in the frame from the knees up | the man on screen left throws a right hook: the first visible motion is the punch itself | real time |
| 3 | 4 to 6 s | Contact insert with a ramp on contact, 85 mm (hero) | locked and tight on the point of contact; at the frame of contact the frame kicks a hand's width in the direction of the force, then settles; exactly side-on to the line of the force, at chest height, 2 m away, never in front of a face | the knuckles stop on the side of the jaw and the cheek gives | slow motion on the approach, then real speed from the exact frame of contact: the reaction happens at full speed and the shot ends when the reaction ends |
| 4 | 6 to 8 s | medium shot, 50 mm | handheld, three-quarter, small late reframing | the head turns away from the fist, the shoulders follow, he takes one step back, and the cut comes when he stops | real time |
| 5 | 8 to 10 s | wide shot, 24 mm | locked, low at knee height, side-on | both men still again: one a step back on screen right with a hand on his jaw, one with his fist still up | real time |
