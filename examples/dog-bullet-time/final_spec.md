Picked: option B. Mixed in: light from C.

| Camera placement | on the floor, 3 m away, the lens level and looking along the ground into the light |
| Lens | 18 mm. An ultra-wide lens on the floor makes the ground rush to the lens and gives a small jump real scale. |

| Length | 2 to 4 s |

## Watch for

- Do not tilt the lens up at the face: it bends bodies and generators stretch them. Keep the lens level.
- Slow motion plus a jump invites floating. Write a real height and real gravity.

## Prompt block

```text
SHOT: Low-angle wide shot. 16:9 frame, 2 to 4 s.
SUBJECT AND ACTION: a scruffy brown dog at the top of a jump, about to catch a thrown bone, in a backyard lawn at sunset.
CAMERA MOVE: locked: the camera does not move, the action comes to it.
CAMERA PLACEMENT: on the floor, 3 m away, the lens level and looking along the ground into the light.
LENS: 18 mm.
LIGHT: low, hard late-afternoon sun from one side only; one side of the subject lit, the other side in real shadow; long hard-edged shadows across the ground.
COLOUR: bleach-bypass look: high contrast, low saturation, silvery highlights, dense blacks, visible grain.
SPEED: slow motion for this one beat only; real gravity, weight and cloth move truthfully, nothing floats.
RULES: State the exact number of people. Screen direction stays the same for the whole shot.
AVOID: not a default eye-level medium shot; no soft even glow; one motivated light source with a real dark side; nobody looks into the lens; every eyeline goes to a target inside the scene; no slow floating camera drift; the camera is locked or makes one decisive move with a clear end; real gravity and real jump height, nobody floats; no bloom, no haze, no fog, no volumetric god rays; worn real textures with dust, creases and small defects, nothing plastic or perfectly clean.
```
