# Present

Read this file when you show the options to the user.

Show each option with its image, or its text diagram, and its previs stills when they exist. Say what the
viewer feels, the pro term, the 6 decisions and the main risk. Mark the option you recommend and say why in
1 sentence. [The option cards](../quality/option-cards.md) show the full card. Use a shorter card when the conversation is short.
