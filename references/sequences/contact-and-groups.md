# Sequences: contacts and groups

Read this file when a shot holds a hit, a catch or a landing, or more than 2 people.

## How to write a contact

Use the shortest description that a generator can draw. For each decisive contact, write 4 things:

1. The part that strikes: "the heel of the hand", "the point of the elbow", "the flat of the bat".
2. The exact target: "the side of the jaw", "the centre of the pad", "the top edge of the ball".
3. The reaction along the line of the force: "the head turns away from the fist, the shoulders follow".
4. The end position on screen: "he ends one step back, on screen right, still on his feet".

For an exchange between equals, use the 4-part cycle: **contact** (the part stops on the target, the surface
gives), **the body goes** (head, shoulders, hips, feet, in that order), **the quarter second** (stillness),
**the return** (the eyes find the other person, the next move starts).

Speed and contact wording that works:

- "The first visible motion is the action itself." This removes the slow wind-up.
- "Out and back in a quarter of a second." "Short straight lines, then stillness."
- "Slow motion on the approach, real speed from the exact frame of contact."
- "At the frame of contact the frame kicks a hand's width in the direction of the force."
- Write the stillness between exchanges. Stillness makes the contact read.
- Show force with deformation, sweat and dust. If the scene does not need injury, write "skin stays clean".

Show each decisive contact 2 times: the whole move in a medium shot, then the contact in a tight insert.
Join the 2 shots on the direction of the force.

Give each person a different way to move. Example: one is precise and fast, the other is heavy and loads
each move visibly. Write who moves first, from which screen side, and who ends where.

## More than 2 people

- Use 4 named people at most in 1 clip. Give each person a distinct description and a screen side.
- Write "exactly 3 people" and "never the same face 2 times".
- Stage the action as 1 against 1, several times. Not 1 against 3 at the same time.
- Give each person in the background a job. People with no job copy each other.
