# Sequences: blocking and references

Read this file when you pick the cheapest blocking method, or attach a reference to a video request.

## Blocking: the cheapest method that works

Start at the lowest level that answers the open question. Go up 1 level only after a draft failed on that question.

| Level | Method | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Words: positions, facing, screen sides, the side the camera stays on | 1 or 2 people, a simple place |
| 2 | A small stage: everything within 2 m of 1 anchor object | Every fight and every contact |
| 3 | The text diagram or the mock still of the option | The framing is the question |
| 4 | A blocking previs ([blocking previs](../previs/index.md)) | The camera path, the lens or the timing must be exact, or 2 drafts placed people wrong |

## References, if the tool accepts them

Give each reference 1 job. Say what the generator takes from it and what it ignores.

| Reference | Takes | Ignores |
|---|---|---|
| An empty picture of the place | Geometry, materials, light | Camera angle and framing |
| An overhead diagram or a previs frame | Start positions, camera position | Look, colour, shapes |
| A good take, as a video | Action and timing | Camera, framing, cuts |

Write the person count again when you attach a reference with several views of 1 person.
