# AI-slop tells: lens, motion and finish

Read this file when you check the lens, the speed or the grade: each tell, why it reads as AI, and the craft fix.

## 3. Lens

| The tell | Why it reads as AI | The craft fix |
|---|---|---|
| **The 35 to 50 mm default at chest height.** | A normal lens from a normal height gives no feeling. | Select the lens for a feeling. 18 to 24 mm, low and close, for scale and energy. 85 to 135 mm, from far away, for compression and tension. Use 35 to 50 mm only as a decision, and then move the camera off the default height or angle. |
| **Fake creamy bokeh.** A sharp cut-out figure on a smeared background. | Real focus falls off with distance. A cut-out edge looks like a phone filter. | State the lens and the distance. Write "focus falls off naturally with distance". Keep shapes readable in the background. |
| **Everything in focus.** | A flat, video-game frame. Nothing guides the eye. | State what is sharp. With a wide lens, use light, not blur, to guide the eye. |
| **Wide-angle face distortion.** A wide lens tilted up at a close face. | Bodies bend and generators stretch them more. | Keep a wide lens level. Keep faces away from the frame edge. For a face, go back and use a long lens. |
| **A zoom when a move was meant.** | A zoom has no parallax. A push-in has parallax. Generators mix them. | Say which one: "the camera itself travels, the background slides past" or "locked camera, the lens zooms, no parallax". |

## 4. Motion and speed

| The tell | Why it reads as AI | The craft fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Aquarium speed.** The camera drifts, and people move as if under water. | Real action is fast, then still. An even slow drift is the signature of generated video. | Use a locked frame, or 1 fast, motivated move with a clear start and end. Write real-time action. Write the stillness between actions. |
| **A camera that never stops moving.** | A move with no reason and no end has no meaning. | 1 move in each shot, for 1 reason. Many shots can be locked. |
| **Slow motion everywhere.** | When every shot is slow, no shot is special. Slow bodies also float. | Real time is the rule. Keep slow motion for 1 beat in the whole piece. Say where it starts and ends. Prefer a speed ramp. |
| **Floating, weightless bodies.** | Generated jumps go too high and hang too long. | Write a real, modest height and "real gravity, no floating". Make a small jump heroic with a low camera, a wide lens and a cut at the top. |
| **One long, slow take.** | The piece has no rhythm. | Cut on movement. For energy, use many short shots and change the camera mode in each shot: floor-level wide, long-lens profile, overhead, detail. |
| **Slow wind-up.** Each action starts with a long preparation. | Real fast action starts at once. | Write "the first visible motion is the action itself". |

## 5. Texture, colour and finish

| The tell | Why it reads as AI | The craft fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Perfect, clean textures.** New floors, plastic skin, flawless metal. | Real places are worn. A perfect surface looks rendered. | Write wear: dust, creases, scratches, small structural defects. |
| **Over-saturated HDR look.** | Glowing colour and detail in every pixel is a showroom finish. | Delete quality words such as "8k", "ultra detailed", "masterpiece". Describe a capture look: fine grain, true blacks, restrained saturation. |
| **Teal and orange by default.** | Orange skin on blue-green shadows is the answer to the word "cinematic". | Select the grade from the story: film print with deep blacks, cool and desaturated, monochrome, muted earth tones. Use teal and orange only on purpose. |
| **"Cinematic", "epic", "dramatic" with no direction.** | Mood words carry no camera, lens or light. The generator answers with all its defaults together. | Replace each mood word with 4 decisions: camera position, lens, light source, speed. |
